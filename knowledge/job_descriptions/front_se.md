# Front SE (Client On-site System Engineer) - Japan

## Position Overview
As a Front System Engineer (Front SE), you will serve as an on-site System Engineer based in Japan, sitting directly at our Japanese clients' offices. Your primary responsibility is to interface directly with the client's business stakeholders and engineering team to manage technical design, clarify specifications, troubleshoot systems, and coordinate delivery.

## Key Requirements
- **Core Focus**:
  - Direct client-facing interface: working on-site (sitting at the client's office in Japan).
  - Translating client requests into system designs and acting as the direct technical point of contact.
- **Language Proficiency**:
  - Japanese: JLPT N3 or N2 (Must be comfortable in direct oral communication, handling client meetings, and documenting requirements).
- **Technical Skills**:
  - General software engineering background (Backend, Frontend, or Cloud Systems).
  - Excellent debugging, system analysis, and API integration skills.
- **Experience**:
  - 2+ years of System Engineering or technical client coordination experience.
