# Business Analyst (BA) - High Level Design

## Position Overview
As a Business Analyst (BA), you will focus on high-level system design, gathering client requirements, and translating business processes into logical architectures. You will work closely with Japanese clients to formulate specifications, mockups, and functional designs.

## Key Requirements
- **Core Focus**:
  - High Level Design (HLD) creation and logical system architecture formulation.
  - Requirement gathering, business process modeling, and functional specification documentation.
- **Language Proficiency**:
  - Japanese: JLPT N1 or N2 (Must be comfortable in meetings, drafting spec documents, and negotiations).
- **Technical Skills**:
  - Solid understanding of software development lifecycle, web applications, databases, and APIs.
  - Proficiency with modeling tools (UML, diagrams, wireframing).
- **Experience**:
  - 3+ years of BA experience, preferably within Japanese IT outsourcing or offshore consulting.
