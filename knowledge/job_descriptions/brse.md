# Bridge System Engineer (BrSE) - Japan Outsourcing

## Position Overview
As a Bridge System Engineer (BrSE), you will serve as the primary conduit between our Japanese clients and our offshore engineering teams. You will manage communications, refine requirements, translate specs, and ensure on-time delivery of high-quality systems.

## Key Requirements
- **Language Proficiency**:
  - Japanese: JLPT N1 or N2 (Fluent in reading, writing, speaking).
  - Able to negotiate technical details, handle meetings, and write report emails to Japanese stakeholders.
- **Technical Skills**:
  - Background in software engineering (formerly Backend, Frontend, or Fullstack).
  - Deep understanding of software design, database schemas, and architectural patterns.
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Translate Japanese client specifications and design sheets into detail tickets (Jira/Confluence) for developers.
  - Coordinate sprint progress, manage schedules, and review deliverables against customer standards.
  - Mitigate project risks and resolve client concerns proactively.
- **Experience**:
  - 2+ years of experience as a BrSE or Technical Lead in an IT outsourcing context.
